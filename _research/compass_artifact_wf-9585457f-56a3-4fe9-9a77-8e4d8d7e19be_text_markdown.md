# Comprehensive Portfolio Strategy for Makimiso Digital Solutions

## The research-informed design advantage

Makimiso Digital Solutions stands at a unique intersection in the digital agency landscape. Unlike traditional agencies that separate research and design, your combination of Paolo's 15+ years of UX expertise with Jarrett's social research background creates an opportunity to position yourselves as the premier **research-informed design agency for mission-driven organizations**.

The market analysis reveals a significant gap: while agencies like IDEO.org excel at design thinking and Media Cause delivers data-driven marketing results, no boutique agency currently combines rigorous social research methodology with UX design expertise specifically for nonprofits and educational institutions. This positioning allows Makimiso to command premium rates ($125-175/hour) while filling a clear market need.

## Portfolio architecture that showcases dual expertise

Your portfolio structure should immediately communicate this integrated approach through a carefully crafted information architecture:

**Primary Navigation:**
- **Impact Stories** - Lead with outcomes, not process
- **Our Approach** - Research + Design methodology  
- **Sectors We Serve** - Education, Nonprofits, Community Organizations

- **About** - The story behind your unique partnership

The homepage should feature a clear value proposition: "We design digital experiences that amplify social impact through rigorous research and human-centered design." This immediately differentiates you from both pure design shops and traditional research consultancies.

## Case study framework that demonstrates methodology and impact

Based on extensive analysis of successful agencies serving similar markets, I recommend adopting a **Research-Forward Impact Narrative** structure for your case studies:

**Executive Summary (10%)**: Start with measurable impact - the numbers that matter to nonprofit decision-makers. Include beneficiaries reached, efficiency gains, or fundraising improvements.

**Challenge & Context (15%)**: Frame the problem within the broader social context, demonstrating deep sector understanding while establishing the stakes.

**Research Methodology (25%)**: This is your key differentiator. Document participatory research approaches, stakeholder interviews, data analysis methods, and how community insights shaped design decisions. Include visual artifacts like journey maps or research synthesis boards.

**Design Process & Outcomes (35%)**: Show the translation from research insights to design solutions. Include wireframes, prototypes, and iterative improvements based on user testing. Demonstrate both aesthetic excellence and functional problem-solving.

**Impact Measurement (25%)**: Present both quantitative metrics (engagement rates, conversion improvements) and qualitative outcomes (stakeholder testimonials, behavioral changes). Include Theory of Change visualizations that nonprofit funders expect.

## Protecting sensitive work while telling compelling stories

Working with vulnerable populations requires careful content management. Implement a three-tier approach:

**Tier 1 - Public Portfolio**: Anonymized case studies using generic descriptors ("Regional Education Foundation," "Youth Development Organization"). Focus on methodology and process rather than specific organizational details. Use illustration or stock photography instead of actual participant photos.

**Tier 2 - Password-Protected**: More detailed case studies available to qualified prospects, still maintaining participant privacy but showing deeper process documentation.

**Tier 3 - Interview-Only**: Your most sensitive or recent work discussed only in person with serious prospects, using presentation decks rather than published materials.

For all tiers, develop composite case studies that combine learnings from multiple projects, allowing you to showcase expertise without violating confidentiality.

## Launch strategy with 3-5 flagship case studies

For your initial portfolio launch, quality trumps quantity. Develop:

- **2 Detailed Case Studies**: Full research-to-implementation narratives showing your complete process
- **3 Project Snapshots**: Shorter format highlighting specific capabilities or outcomes
- **1 Pro Bono/Passion Project**: Demonstrates mission alignment and allows creative freedom

Supplement these with process documentation, thought leadership content, and methodology explanations that establish your expertise even with limited client work to showcase initially.
