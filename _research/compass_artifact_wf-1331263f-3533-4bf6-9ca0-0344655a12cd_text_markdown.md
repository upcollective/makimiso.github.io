# Portfolio Strategy and Case Study Recommendations for Makimiso Digital Solutions

## Executive Summary

Based on comprehensive research of Jarrett Davis and Paolo Austria's backgrounds, this report identifies optimal portfolio projects and strategic approaches for positioning Makimiso Digital Solutions as the premier research-informed design agency for nonprofits and educational institutions. The analysis reveals extraordinary complementary expertise that creates a unique market position: Jarrett's 15+ years of deep social research with vulnerable populations combined with Paolo's extensive experience in human-centered design for major brands creates an unmatched value proposition for mission-driven organizations.

## Strategic Portfolio Recommendations

### Priority Case Studies (Ranked by Impact Potential)

#### 1. **"From Research to Reality: The Disrupting Harm Digital Platform"**
*Showcasing Research-Informed Design at Scale*
- **Background**: Leverage Jarrett's INTERPOL/UNICEF research leadership
- **Narrative**: Transform complex multi-country research data into accessible digital tools for child protection organizations
- **Deliverables to Highlight**:
  - Interactive data dashboards for frontline workers
  - Mobile-responsive reporting systems
  - Multilingual interface design considerations
  - Trauma-informed UX patterns
- **Impact Metrics**: Countries served, organizations reached, policy changes influenced
- **Why It Resonates**: Demonstrates ability to handle sensitive topics with both research rigor and design excellence

#### 2. **"Community Voices: Participatory Design for Cambodian Youth Services"**
*Demonstrating Authentic Community Engagement*
- **Background**: Build on Jarrett's extensive Cambodia fieldwork
- **Narrative**: Co-design digital services with street-involved youth and service providers
- **Process Documentation**:
  - Participatory workshop photos
  - User journey maps created with community input
  - Prototype testing with target populations
  - Iterative design based on field feedback
- **Unique Value**: Shows deep cultural competency and ethical research practices
- **Measurable Outcomes**: Service adoption rates, user satisfaction, reduced barriers to access

#### 3. **"Visualizing Impact: Love146 Multi-Country Research Dashboard"**
*Data Visualization & Impact Communication Excellence*
- **Foundation**: Jarrett's multi-year vulnerability studies across 4 countries
- **Design Challenge**: Make complex research accessible to donors, policymakers, and field teams
- **Key Features**:
  - Geographic data visualization
  - Trend analysis interfaces
  - Story integration with quantitative data
  - Mobile-first design for field access
- **Results**: Funding increases, program expansion, stakeholder engagement metrics

#### 4. **"Digital Transformation for Survivor Support: The Butterfly Project Portal"**
*Ethical AI and Digital Strategy Integration*
- **Context**: 10-year longitudinal study digital infrastructure
- **Innovation Points**:
  - Privacy-first design for sensitive data
  - AI-powered pattern recognition for early intervention
  - Culturally appropriate interface design
  - Sustainable technology choices for resource-limited settings
- **Impact**: Long-term tracking capabilities, improved survivor outcomes, research efficiency

#### 5. **"Breaking Brand Boundaries: Accessible Design Systems for Social Impact"**
*Leveraging Paolo's Enterprise Experience for Nonprofits*
- **Concept**: Adapt Fortune 500 design excellence to nonprofit constraints
- **Case Examples**:
  - Scalable design systems for resource-limited organizations
  - Cost-effective brand development processes
  - Accessibility-first approach learned from major brands
  - Open-source design resources for the sector
- **Value Proposition**: Enterprise-quality design at nonprofit budgets

3. **Geographic Limitations**: Emphasize successful remote collaboration and global project management
4. **Sensitive Topic Handling**: Develop clear ethical guidelines and trauma-informed design principles
